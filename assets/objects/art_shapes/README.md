# Art Shapes

Generated graspable primitive plastic pieces for RoboLab art/drawing completion tasks.

- Thickness: 1.5 cm
- Edge bevel/chamfer: 1.5 mm
- Materials: uploaded ambientCG Plastic007/008/011/013A/014A/015A/016A/017A 1K PBR maps
- Class: `art_shape`
- Dataset: `art_shapes`
- Collision metadata: dynamic rigid body; solid shapes use convex-hull mesh collision, hollow circles use SDF mesh collision to preserve the open center

## Shapes

- `rectangle_2x2`: 2×2 cm rectangle/square
- `rectangle_2x4`: 2×4 cm rectangle
- `rectangle_2x6`: 2×6 cm rectangle
- `rectangle_2x10`: 2×10 cm rectangle
- `rectangle_2x16`: 2×16 cm rectangle
- `rectangle_2x32`: 2×32 cm rectangle
- `circle_4`: 4 cm circle
- `hollow_circle_6`: 6 cm hollow circle with 1.5 cm outline
- `hollow_circle_10`: 10 cm hollow circle with 1.5 cm outline
- `hollow_circle_14`: 14 cm hollow circle with 1.5 cm outline
- `hollow_circle_20`: 20 cm hollow circle with 1.5 cm outline
- `hollow_circle_30`: 30 cm hollow circle with 1.5 cm outline
- `wavy_rectangle_2x20`: 2×20 cm wavy strip
- `triangle_equilateral_4`: 4 cm equilateral triangle
- `triangle_right_4x4`: 4×4 cm right triangle

## Colors

- `red_orange`: `Plastic007_1K-PNG`
- `dark_blue`: `Plastic008_1K-PNG`
- `charcoal`: `Plastic011_1K-PNG`
